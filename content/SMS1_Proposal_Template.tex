\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
Educational institutions increasingly require digital solutions to efficiently manage academic activities, monitor student performance, and maintain accurate records. This proposal presents a Smart Academic Management System with AI-Based Attendance and Performance Analytics.

\tableofcontents

\chapter{INTRODUCTION}
\section{Project Overview}
Educational institutions manage a large volume of academic information, including student records, course allocations, attendance, assignments, notices, and performance evaluations.

\section{Problem Statement}
Educational institutions often face challenges in managing academic operations efficiently due to fragmented systems and manual attendance processes.

\section{Project Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Implement secure RBAC.
\item Manage departments, semesters, courses, teachers and students.
\item Develop AI-powered attendance using YOLOv8.
\item Provide assignment management.
\item Generate performance analytics and GPA prediction.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Significance of the Study}
The proposed system improves efficiency and effectiveness of academic management processes.

\section{Scope and Limitations}
Role-based access control, attendance tracking, analytics, and GPA prediction are included.
